An intelligent, two-way approach to lateral partner search. Beyond the numbers. Beyond the resume. Beyond conventional recruitment.

Search with greater precision. Partner value deserves a deeper view.

For law firms defining their next strategic hire and partners considering their next professional chapter, better matching starts with better information. The strongest partnerships are built on a deeper understanding of fit, value and opportunity.

Why It Works

For Law Firms

Define your search. Discover relevant talent.

Specify the requirements that matter. AI-driven search agents help analyse and prioritise law firm requirements, allowing you to focus on the right prospective partner profile.

For Partners

Understand where your profile fits.

Assess how your practice and commercial credentials align with opportunities that may be a strong strategic fit, while keeping your confidentiality and professional priorities front and centre.

For Law Firms

Hire for the practice you want to build.

Identify partners whose expertise, relationships and ambitions align with your strategic hiring priorities and the long-term shape of your practice.

For Partners

Make your next move count.

Your value is not defined by one number. It reflects the practice you've built, the clients you serve, the strategic opportunities you can unlock and the fit with the right platform.

One market. Two perspectives. Smarter alignment.

That same intelligence can help law firms identify the right partner profile while also helping partners understand where their expertise, relationships and commercial value align with the right opportunity.
