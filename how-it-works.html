A smarter search. From brief to introduction.

A more considered approach to lateral partner search, connecting firm requirements with partner expertise, commercial credentials, and future ambitions.

  1. 01 — Define

    Start with the strategy.

    Establish practice requirements, target markets, commercial expectations, and long-term objectives. AI-assisted tools help structure the brief and identify the criteria that matter most.

  2. 02 — Discover

    Let intelligence find the alignment.

    AI-driven search agents analyze relevant firm requirements and partner profiles to surface potential matches across expertise, geography, commercial credentials, and strategic fit.

  3. 03 — Evaluate

    Put the insight into context.

    Review relevant experience, potential alignment, and available evidence. Distinguish verified facts from self-reported information and outstanding questions.

  4. 04 — Shortlist

    Focus on the right conversations.

    Explore targeted, insight-led shortlists with clear matching rationale and the factors worth investigating further.

  5. 05 — Connect

    Make the right introduction.

    Progress relevant opportunities through a discreet, consent-led process. Identifying information is shared only in accordance with the agreed disclosure arrangements.

Intelligent technology. Considered decisions.
