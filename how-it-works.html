How It Works

  1. 01 — Simply search for a strategic requirement or priority hire

    Law firms and businesses outline their needs confidentially in a free-to-access, simple one-line search.

    Include expertise, seniority, industry experience, geography, commercial metrics and strategic objectives.

  2. 02 — Discover relevant prospects through LateralSmart's AI intelligence platform

    Our platform analyses relevant data to identify, assess and rank a limited number of potential Partners.

    Converting your simple search into actionable insights based on experience, expertise and professional suitability.

  3. 03 — Explore relevant anonymized profiles confidentially

    Review anonymised Partner profiles, date and intelligence before identities are disclosed.

    LateralSmart's AI-driven platform enables informed decisions while protecting professional confidentiality.

  4. 04 — Request a consent-led introduction

    When a law firm or business wishes to engage with a prospective Partner, the platform can facilitate a confidential approach at your direction.

    A secure flagging system enables you to establish interest and obtain relevant consents before either party sharies their identity or details.

  5. 05 — Connect securely and with confidence

    Once consent has been granted, the Partner’s identity and authorised details are disclosed.

    LateralSmart's clever technology then unlocks and enables direct engagement allowing parties to progress through a recruitment process if that is what they wish to do.

  6. 06 — Manage your own process and complete a valuable hire

    The law firm or business manages the interview, negotiation and appointment process.

    LateralSmart's pure focus is on providing the parties with useful intelligence and facilitating valuable introductions through the platform. The rest is up to you.

  7. 07 — Transparent success-based fees

    An agreed introduction and fee arrangement in place between LateralSmart and our law firms and business users governs each engagement.

    Standard platform fees are applicable unless bespoke arrangements have been agreed by separate negotiation. Where a partner is appointed as a result of a qualifying introduction, the applicable recruitment fee becomes payable in accordance with the agreed terms.

Confidential by design. Transparent by agreement.

Our model combines AI-driven talent intelligence, controlled identity disclosure, recorded consent and clear commercial terms. Partners retain control over whether their identities are shared, while law firms and businesses benefit from a structured, accountable path to being introduced to and hiring relevant, valuable, exceptional talent.

All introductions, data disclosures and fee arrangements are subject to applicable data protection requirements and the platform’s agreed terms.
