How It Works Why It Works Strategic Partners

Free-to-search, AI-driven insights changing the way leaders of law firms, global businesses and law firm Partners find each other.

Organizations search anonymously. Anonymized Partners see the demand. If mutual interest exists, a simple, consent-led reveal process begins.

Trusted by law firms, global businesses and Partners alike, LateralSmart's AI-driven data-led search platform gives you the option of entering a discreet introduction process.

How It Works for law firms and organizations

  1. 01 — Simply search for a strategic requirement or priority hire

    Law firms and businesses outline their needs confidentially in a free-to-access, simple one-line search.

    Include expertise, seniority, industry experience, geography, commercial metrics and strategic objectives.

  2. 02 — Discover relevant prospects through LateralSmart's AI intelligence platform

    Our platform analyses relevant data to identify, assess and rank a limited number of potential Partners.

    Converting your simple search into actionable insights based on experience, expertise and professional suitability.

  3. 03 — Explore relevant anonymized profiles confidentially

    Review anonymised Partner profiles, data and intelligence before identities are disclosed.

    LateralSmart's AI-driven platform enables informed decisions while protecting professional confidentiality.

  4. 04 — Request a consent-led introduction

    When a law firm or business wishes to engage with a prospective Partner, the platform can facilitate a confidential approach at your direction.

    A secure flagging system enables you to establish interest and obtain relevant consents before either party shares their identity or details.

How It Works for Partners

  1. 01 — Define your expertise and strategic ambitions

    Confidentially outline your expertise, experience and ambitions to help LateralSmart identify relevant opportunities.

    Share your specialisms, seniority, industry experience, geography, commercial achievements and strategic priorities.

  2. 02 — Be identified through LateralSmart’s AI intelligence platform

    Our platform analyses relevant data to assess your professional profile against suitable Partner opportunities.

    Matching your expertise, experience and commercial track record to the strategic requirements of law firms and businesses.

  3. 03 — Maintain control over your professional identity

    Your anonymised profile and relevant intelligence can inform opportunity matching before your identity is disclosed.

    LateralSmart’s AI-driven platform supports informed consideration of relevant opportunities while protecting your professional confidentiality.

  4. 04 — Choose whether to engage with an opportunity

    When a law firm or business expresses interest, you retain control over whether to proceed with a confidential introduction.

    A secure flagging system enables interest and relevant consents to be established before either party shares their identity or details.

Confidential by design. Transparent by agreement.

Our model combines AI-driven talent intelligence, controlled identity disclosure, recorded consent and clear commercial terms. Partners retain control over whether and how they engage, while organizations gain access to relevant, appropriately matched opportunities.
